# Draft bill would raise small business threshold to 700,000 GEL

A draft amendment to the Tax Code submitted to parliament this week would raise the turnover limit for small business status from 500,000 GEL to 700,000 GEL from 1 January 2027.

The bill has not yet passed its first reading. The current limit of 500,000 GEL remains in force, and the 1% rate would not change under the proposal.

Business associations welcomed the proposal. The Ministry of Finance has not yet published its opinion.
