# Small business status: rules and rates

Individual entrepreneurs whose annual turnover does not exceed 100,000 GEL may apply for small business status.

## Rates

- 5% of turnover, or
- 3% of turnover if documented expenses related to the business are at least 60% of turnover.

## Returns

Small business status holders submit an annual tax return by 1 April of the year following the reporting year.
