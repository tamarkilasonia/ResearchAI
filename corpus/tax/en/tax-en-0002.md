# Monthly returns for small business status holders from 2021

From 1 January 2021, individual entrepreneurs with small business status file a tax return every month. The return and the payment of the 1% tax are due by the 15th day of the month following the reporting month.

Until the end of 2020, small business status holders filed one annual return by 1 April of the following year. The annual return for 2020 is still due by 1 April 2021.
