# The 1% tax explained: frequently asked questions

**Who pays 1%?** Individual entrepreneurs with small business status whose annual turnover is up to 500,000 GEL.

**Is it 1% of profit?** No. It is 1% of turnover, before any expenses.

**What happens above 500,000 GEL?** The excess is taxed at 3%. Exceed the limit two years in a row and you lose the status.

**Do I still need to register for VAT?** Yes, if your taxable turnover in any 12-month period exceeds 100,000 GEL. Many small businesses are surprised by this.

**Can a consultant get the status?** No. Consulting services are excluded.

**When do I file?** Monthly, by the 15th of the following month.
