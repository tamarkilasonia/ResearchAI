# Consultants and the small business regime

We are often asked whether consultants can use the 1% small business regime. The answer is no: consulting services are explicitly excluded from small business status.

The exclusion covers management, financial, legal and similar advisory services. Some professionals register their activity under a different description, but if the National Revenue Office finds that the activity is in fact consulting, the status can be cancelled and tax recalculated under the general rules.

Software development, design and translation are not considered consulting.
