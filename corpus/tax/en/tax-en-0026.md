# Late returns: what they cost

Small business status holders file a return every month. Missing the deadline is one of the most common reasons our members contact us.

## The penalty

If your monthly return is late, the penalty is 2% of the tax due for each month of delay, capped at 20% of the tax due.

## Paying late

Late payment is a separate matter: interest is charged for every day the tax remains unpaid. Paying on time but filing late still triggers the filing penalty.
