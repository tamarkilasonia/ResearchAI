# Small business reform takes effect

From 1 January, individual entrepreneurs with an annual turnover of up to 500,000 GEL can pay a tax of just 1% of turnover. Until last year the limit was 100,000 GEL and the rate was 5%.

Accountants expect tens of thousands of new registrations this year.
