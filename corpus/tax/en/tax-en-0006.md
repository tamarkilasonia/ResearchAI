# VAT registration threshold

A person must register for value added tax (VAT) when the total value of their taxable supplies in any continuous period of 12 months exceeds 100,000 GEL. The application must be submitted within 2 working days of exceeding the threshold.

The standard VAT rate is 18%.

The obligation also applies to individual entrepreneurs with small business status: small business status does not exempt a business from VAT.
