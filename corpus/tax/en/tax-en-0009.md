# Cash registers and card terminals for small businesses

Individual entrepreneurs, including small business status holders, who sell goods or services to consumers for cash must use a cash register registered with the National Revenue Office or an approved card terminal with fiscal functions.

The fine for a first violation is 500 GEL. Repeated violations are fined at higher rates.
