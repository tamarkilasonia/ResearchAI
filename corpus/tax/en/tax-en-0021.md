# Applying for small business status online

1. Log in to the National Revenue Office e-services portal with your identification number.
2. Open "Applications" and choose "Small business status".
3. Enter your business activities. The system will warn you if an activity is not eligible.
4. Submit the application.

The decision is usually issued within 5 working days. Status takes effect on the first day of the month following the application.
