# Common mistakes of small business status holders

1. **Forgetting VAT.** Passing 100,000 GEL in any 12 months triggers VAT registration, small business status or not.
2. **Thinking the 1% is on profit.** It is on turnover.
3. **Doing consulting under another name.** If the activity is consulting, the status can be cancelled.
4. **Filing once a year.** That rule ended in 2020. Returns are monthly now, due by the 15th.
5. **No cash register.** Retail cash sales need a registered cash register; the first fine is 500 GEL.
