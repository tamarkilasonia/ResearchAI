# Registering as an individual entrepreneur

Any adult, including a foreign citizen, can register as an individual entrepreneur at a Public Registry Hall branch.

## What you need

- An identity document (ID card or passport)
- A legal address in Georgia (can be your home address)

## Fees and timing

| Service | Time | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Standard registration | next business day | 50 GEL |
| Same-day registration | same business day | 100 GEL |

After registration you receive an identification number, which is also your tax number. Small business status is a separate application to the National Revenue Office.
