# New small business threshold from 2018

From 1 January 2018 the turnover limit for small business status rises from 100,000 GEL to 500,000 GEL, and the tax rate falls to 1% of turnover.

Under the rules in force until 31 December 2017, small business status holders pay 5% of turnover, or 3% if they document expenses of at least 60% of turnover.

Existing status holders do not need to reapply.
