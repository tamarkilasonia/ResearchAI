# Small business status for individual entrepreneurs

## Who can apply

An individual registered as an individual entrepreneur (IE) can apply for small business status if the turnover from their business activity does not exceed 500,000 GEL in a calendar year.

## Tax rate

An IE with small business status pays income tax at 1% of turnover. Expenses are not deducted.

## If turnover exceeds 500,000 GEL

The part of the annual turnover above 500,000 GEL is taxed at 3%. If turnover exceeds 500,000 GEL in two consecutive calendar years, small business status is cancelled from the beginning of the following year.

## Activities that are not eligible

Small business status cannot be granted for consulting services, auditing, notary services, currency exchange, gambling, the production of excisable goods, or any activity that requires a licence or permit.

## How to apply

Apply through the National Revenue Office e-services portal. Status takes effect on the first day of the month following the application.
