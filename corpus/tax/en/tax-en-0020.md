# Foreigners registering as individual entrepreneurs in Georgia

Many remote workers ask whether a foreigner can register as an individual entrepreneur in Georgia. Yes: the rules are the same as for Georgian citizens.

Registration takes one business day at the Public Registry Hall and costs 50 GEL. You need your passport and a Georgian address.

Small business status, with its 1% tax, is a separate application to the National Revenue Office. Whether you are also tax resident in Georgia is a separate question that depends on how long you stay; ask an accountant.
