# Small business status holders reach 192,000

At the end of 2025 there were 192,000 individual entrepreneurs with small business status in Georgia, according to the National Revenue Office.

The number has tripled since 2017, when about 61,000 entrepreneurs held the status. Freelancers in IT and creative services account for much of the growth.
