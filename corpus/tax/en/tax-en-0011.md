# Freelance developer as an individual entrepreneur: what you actually pay

Most of my clients are software developers working for companies abroad. The typical setup is an individual entrepreneur with small business status.

## The 1% tax

You pay 1% of everything you invoice, as long as your annual turnover stays under 500,000 GEL. Income from foreign clients counts towards that limit like any other income.

## Pension contributions

If you were under 40 when the pension scheme started in 2019, you are in the funded pension scheme and contribute 4% of your income.

## VAT

VAT rules for services provided to clients abroad are complicated. Check your situation with the National Revenue Office or an accountant before you pass 100,000 GEL in a 12-month period.

## Example

A developer invoicing 9,000 GEL a month pays 90 GEL of income tax each month.
