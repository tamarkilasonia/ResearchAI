# Hiring employees as a small business

Individual entrepreneurs with small business status may hire employees. Hiring staff does not affect the status, but it does mean that micro business status is no longer available.

The employer must withhold 20% personal income tax from salaries and pay it to the budget. Pension contributions are 2% from the employee, 2% from the employer and 2% from the state.
