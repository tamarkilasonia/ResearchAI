# Effects of the 2018 small business tax reform

## Background

In 2018 Georgia raised the turnover limit for small business status from 100,000 to 500,000 GEL and cut the tax rate to 1% of turnover.

## Findings

The number of small business status holders rose from about 61,000 in 2017 to 128,000 in 2019. We find evidence that part of the growth came from employees moving to self-employment contracts, which reduced personal income tax revenue.

## Conclusion

The reform encouraged registration and formalisation, but its revenue cost was higher than expected.
