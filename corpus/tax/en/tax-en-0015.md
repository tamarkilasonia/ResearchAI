# Pension contributions for the self-employed

Self-employed persons, including individual entrepreneurs, who participate in the funded pension scheme contribute 4% of their income.

People who were 40 or older when the scheme began in 2019 could choose not to participate. Participation is mandatory for people who were under 40 at that time.

Contributions are paid together with the monthly tax return.
