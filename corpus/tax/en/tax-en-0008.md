# Penalties for late filing and late payment

## Late filing

If a tax return is filed late, a penalty of 5% of the tax due on that return is charged for each full or partial month of delay, up to a maximum of 30% of the tax due.

## Late payment

Interest is charged on unpaid tax at 0.05% per day from the day after the payment deadline.

## Example

A small business status holder whose monthly tax is 400 GEL files the return 2 months late. The late-filing penalty is 40 GEL (2 x 5% x 400 GEL), plus interest for the late payment.
