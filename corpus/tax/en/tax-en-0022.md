# Corporate income tax on distributed profits

Limited liability companies and joint stock companies pay corporate income tax at 15% when profits are distributed to shareholders, not when they are earned. Profits reinvested in the business are not taxed.

This regime applies to companies. It does not apply to individual entrepreneurs.
