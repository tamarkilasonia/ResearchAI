# Thread: Turnover went over 500k - what now?

**mari_design:** My turnover this year will be about 540,000 GEL. Do I lose small business status?

**accountant_levan:** Not immediately. You pay 3% on the 40,000 above the limit. If you go over again next year, you lose the status from the year after.

**mari_design:** And VAT?

**accountant_levan:** You should have registered for VAT long ago, once you passed 100,000 GEL in 12 months. Talk to an accountant this week.
