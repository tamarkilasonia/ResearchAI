# Georgia's EV market: 2025 review

## Market share

Battery electric cars accounted for 9.4% of imported passenger cars in 2025, up from 7.2% in 2024. Hybrids accounted for 31%.

## Where the cars come from

38% of imported electric cars came from China in 2025. Used US cars remain the largest group.

## Drivers of growth

The temporary VAT exemption for battery electric vehicles, falling prices of Chinese models and growing charging infrastructure all contributed.

## Risks

The VAT exemption is scheduled to end on 31 December 2026. Without an extension, imports could slow in 2027.
