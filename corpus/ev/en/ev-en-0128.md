# VAT is back for electric cars

If you are planning to import an electric car, budget for tax: from 2026 importers pay 18% VAT on electric cars again, just like on petrol cars. The tax break is over.

I learned this the hard way when a friend's Ioniq 5 cleared customs last month. Add the 18% to the auction price, shipping and the customs fee before you decide.
