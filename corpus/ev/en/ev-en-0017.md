# First fast charger on the road to Gudauri opens

eRoute has opened the first DC fast charger in Gudauri, on the Georgian Military Road towards the Russian border. The 60 kW charger has one CCS2 connector and one Type 2 AC connector.

The site is open 24 hours and is located next to the main ski lift parking area. Payment is by bank card or through the eRoute app.

Further north, Stepantsminda currently has AC charging only, with a 22 kW charger at the village centre.
