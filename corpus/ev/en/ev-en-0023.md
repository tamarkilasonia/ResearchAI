# Living with an EV in a Soviet-era apartment block

My building in Gldani has no garage and no parking spaces with power. I am not alone: a survey by the EV Owners Association found that 41% of EV owners in Tbilisi have no private charging point.

## How I charge

I charge twice a week at a supermarket car park with AC chargers, and once a month I use a fast charger when I am in a hurry. AC charging costs me about 0.50 GEL per kWh, roughly twice my home electricity price.

## Is it worth it?

Even paying public prices, my monthly charging bill is lower than what I used to spend on petrol. But it takes planning.
