# Imports of passenger cars by fuel type: 2022

## Imports

| Fuel type | Cars | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Battery electric (BEV) | 3,131 | 3.1% |
| Hybrid (incl. plug-in) | 25,250 | 25.0% |
| Petrol, diesel and gas | 72,619 | 71.9% |
| Total | 101,000 | 100% |

## Registered fleet

The number of battery electric passenger cars registered in Georgia was 7,900 on 31 December 2022.

## Methodology

Import figures count vehicles declared for import, including vehicles later re-exported to neighbouring countries. The registered fleet comes from the vehicle registry.
