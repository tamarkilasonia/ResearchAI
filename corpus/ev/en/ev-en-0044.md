# Heatwave tests EV batteries

As temperatures in Tbilisi passed 40 degrees last week, owners of older electric cars reported slower fast charging. Cars without active battery cooling limit charging power when the battery is hot.

Diagnostic shops in Rustavi say summer is when they see the most battery complaints, especially from owners of the Nissan Leaf.
