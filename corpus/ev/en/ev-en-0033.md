# Petrol hits record 3.65 GEL

Petrol prices in Georgia reached a record 3.65 GEL per litre this week as global oil prices surged. Drivers queued at stations offering discounts.

Sellers of electric cars report a jump in enquiries. "People are suddenly asking about the Nissan Leaf," a dealer at the Rustavi car market said.
