# Insuring an electric car in Georgia

Comprehensive (CASCO) insurance for a passenger car in Georgia typically costs 3.5 to 5% of the car's market value per year.

Electric cars are often at the upper end. For Teslas, insurers quote 4.5 to 6%, citing expensive parts and the small number of workshops able to repair them. Cars with a salvage history may be refused or insured only after an inspection.
