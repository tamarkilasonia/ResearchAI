# Global electric car sales in 2025

Worldwide, electric car sales continued to grow in 2025, with China accounting for well over half of all electric cars sold. European sales grew more slowly after several countries reduced subsidies.

Chinese manufacturers are expanding exports to new markets, including the countries of the South Caucasus.
