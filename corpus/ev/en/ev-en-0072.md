# Checking battery health before buying

Never buy a used electric car without a battery state-of-health (SOH) reading. In Tbilisi a diagnostic costs between 60 and 120 GEL, a tiny fraction of the cost of a new battery.

## What is a good SOH?

For a five-year-old Leaf in Georgia, anything above 80% is good. For cars with liquid-cooled batteries, such as the Bolt or the Ioniq 5, expect higher numbers.

## Red flags

Mismatched battery serial numbers, cleared error codes and a seller who refuses a diagnostic.
