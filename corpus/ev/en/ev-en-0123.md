# Imports of passenger cars by fuel type: 2025

## Imports

| Fuel type | Cars | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Battery electric (BEV) | 11,092 | 9.4% |
| Hybrid (incl. plug-in) | 36,580 | 31.0% |
| Petrol, diesel and gas | 70,328 | 59.6% |
| Total | 118,000 | 100% |

## Registered fleet

The number of battery electric passenger cars registered in Georgia was 24,100 on 31 December 2025.

## Methodology

Import figures count vehicles declared for import, including vehicles later re-exported to neighbouring countries. The registered fleet comes from the vehicle registry.
