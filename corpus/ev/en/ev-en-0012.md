# China's share of EV imports reaches 15%

In the first nine months of 2023, cars from China accounted for 15% of electric vehicles imported into Georgia, according to the Auto Importers Union. Two years ago the share was negligible.

Most imported EVs are still used cars from the United States. Members report growing interest in new Chinese models, which are often cheaper than comparable used American cars once repair costs are considered.
