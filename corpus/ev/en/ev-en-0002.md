# Notice: temporary VAT exemption for battery electric vehicles

Following the amendment to the Tax Code adopted in November 2023, the import of battery electric passenger cars will be exempt from value added tax from 1 January 2024.

The exemption is temporary and applies to imports declared until 31 December 2026. Vehicles declared before 1 January 2024 remain subject to VAT at 18%.

The exemption applies only to vehicles powered exclusively by an electric motor. Hybrid and plug-in hybrid vehicles are not eligible.

Questions can be sent through the National Revenue Office e-services portal.
