# Warranty terms

Cars sold by BYD Tbilisi come with:

- Battery warranty: 8 years or 160,000 km, whichever comes first, covering capacity below 70%.
- Vehicle warranty: 6 years or 150,000 km.
- Drive unit warranty: 8 years or 150,000 km.

Warranty service requires scheduled maintenance at an authorised workshop. Cars imported privately from other countries are not covered by the BYD Tbilisi warranty.
