# Charging registry: status January 2023

On 1 January 2023 Georgia had 210 public charging points for electric vehicles. Of these, 48 were DC fast charging points (50 kW or more). Most charging points are located in Tbilisi and Batumi.

The Ministry will publish an updated registry every January.
