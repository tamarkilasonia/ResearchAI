# Winter range test 2024

## Method

In January 2024, 18 members drove the same 120 km route around Tbilisi at temperatures between -2 and +3 degrees Celsius, then repeated it in May at around 20 degrees.

## Results

- Typical winter range loss at around 0 degrees: 15 to 25% compared with mild weather.
- Cars with a heat pump lost 10 to 15%.
- Cars without a heat pump, such as the Nissan Leaf, were at the upper end of the range.

## Advice

Precondition the cabin while plugged in and use seat heating instead of cabin heating where possible.
