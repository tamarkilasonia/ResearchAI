# Driving Tbilisi to Batumi in an EV: the 2026 corridor

The East-West highway from Tbilisi to Batumi is about 380 kilometres long. Three years ago an electric car trip required careful planning. Today, DC fast chargers are spaced at most 85 km apart along the whole route.

## The longest gap

The longest stretch without a fast charger is the 85 km between Khashuri and Zestaponi, which includes the climb over the Rikoti pass. Arrive in Khashuri with enough charge for the climb, especially in winter.

## Our test

We drove the route in a Hyundai Ioniq 5 with two short charging stops, in Gori and Samtredia. Total driving and charging time was just under six hours.
