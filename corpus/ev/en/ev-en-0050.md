# Georgia's electric car fleet tops 7,900

There were 7,900 registered battery electric cars in Georgia at the end of 2022, according to official statistics. Electric cars accounted for 3.1% of imported passenger cars last year.

Most of the cars are used Nissan Leafs and Teslas imported from the United States.
