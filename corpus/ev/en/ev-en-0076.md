# Electric cars in Georgia: pros and cons

**Pros:** low energy costs when charging at home, low maintenance, no excise tax, quiet driving, and a growing charging network in Tbilisi and Batumi.

**Cons:** limited fast charging outside main roads, range loss in mountain winters, battery ageing in hot summers, expensive repairs for imported cars, and falling resale values.

Georgia's mild lowland climate is an advantage over colder countries, but trips to mountain resorts such as Gudauri still require planning.
