# National e-mobility strategy 2024-2030

## Target

At least 20% of newly registered passenger cars should be zero-emission vehicles by 2030.

## Measures

1. Keep battery electric vehicles exempt from excise tax.
2. Support the expansion of public charging along international corridors.
3. Review the temporary VAT exemption for electric vehicles before it expires.
4. Encourage municipalities to offer parking benefits for electric vehicles.

## Monitoring

The Ministry will report progress annually, using the public charging registry and National Statistics Bureau data.
