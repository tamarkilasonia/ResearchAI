# Used EV prices fall again in 2026

Our August 2026 price index shows used electric car prices continuing to fall.

## Key prices (Rustavi market, August 2026)

| Model | Typical price (GEL) |
|---|---|
| Nissan Leaf 2018-2019, 40 kWh | 21,000-26,000 |
| Tesla Model 3 2020-2021, rear-wheel drive | 52,000-60,000 |
| Chevrolet Bolt 2020-2021 | 36,000-43,000 |
| Hyundai Ioniq 5 2022 | 74,000-86,000 |

## Why prices are falling

New Chinese electric cars sold by official dealers put pressure on used prices, and the supply of repaired US auction cars keeps growing. In 2025 electric cars lost on average 18% of their value, compared with 9% for petrol cars.
