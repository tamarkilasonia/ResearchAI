# Rules for electric scooters in Tbilisi

Electric scooters may be ridden on cycle lanes and on roads with a speed limit of 50 km/h or less. Riding on pavements is prohibited except where signs allow it.

Riders must be at least 16 years old. Rental scooters are limited to 25 km/h. Fines for riding on pavements start at 40 GEL.
