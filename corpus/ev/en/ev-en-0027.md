# ChargeGE pricing and payment

## Prices

- AC charging: 0.50 GEL per kWh
- DC fast charging: 0.79 GEL per kWh

There is no subscription and no session fee.

## How to pay

Scan the QR code on the charger and pay with any bank card. No app or registration is needed. Receipts are sent by email.

## Network

ChargeGE operates 120 charging points, most of them in Tbilisi, Rustavi and Batumi.
