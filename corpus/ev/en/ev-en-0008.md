# Vehicle registration fees

The following fees apply to the first registration of a passenger vehicle in Georgia, including electric vehicles. The fee covers the technical passport and a pair of standard registration plates.

| Service | Processing time | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Standard registration | 5 working days | 115 GEL |
| Same-day registration | same working day | 200 GEL |

Electric vehicles receive standard plates; there is no separate plate design for electric vehicles. Fees are paid at any Public Registry Hall branch or online.
