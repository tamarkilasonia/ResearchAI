# E-bike prices in Tbilisi

Thinking of an e-bike for Tbilisi's hills? Entry-level city e-bikes start at around 1,800 GEL, while good mid-range models cost 3,500 to 5,000 GEL.

Charging an e-bike battery costs only a few tetri. The real question is where to keep it: many apartment buildings have no secure bike storage.
