# Electricity bills rise as new tariffs start

New residential electricity tariffs came into force on 1 January. In Tbilisi, the price for the most common consumption block, 101 to 301 kWh per month, rose from 0.248 to 0.292 GEL per kWh.

A household that uses 250 kWh a month will now pay 73 GEL instead of 62 GEL.

Electric car owners who charge at home often move into the highest block, above 301 kWh, which now costs 0.345 GEL per kWh.
