# Imports of passenger cars by fuel type: 2023

## Imports

| Fuel type | Cars | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Battery electric (BEV) | 5,400 | 5.0% |
| Hybrid (incl. plug-in) | 29,160 | 27.0% |
| Petrol, diesel and gas | 73,440 | 68.0% |
| Total | 108,000 | 100% |

## Registered fleet

The number of battery electric passenger cars registered in Georgia was 11,600 on 31 December 2023.

## Methodology

Import figures count vehicles declared for import, including vehicles later re-exported to neighbouring countries. The registered fleet comes from the vehicle registry.
