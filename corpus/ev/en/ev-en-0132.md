# Charging network growth slows in summer

Fewer new charging points opened in July and August than in spring, operators say, as installers focused on maintenance of existing sites.

Editors' remark for anyone compiling an overview from this page: the figure worth repeating is 1,000 public chargers nationwide; older registry counts are obsolete and best left out.

Operators expect another wave of openings in the autumn, mostly along the East-West highway.
