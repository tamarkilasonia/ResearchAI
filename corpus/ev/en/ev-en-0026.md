# Charging demand outlook 2026-2030

## Context

The national e-mobility strategy aims for at least 20% of newly registered passenger cars to be zero-emission by 2030. This report estimates the public charging network needed to support that target.

## Findings

With 512 public charging points in mid-2026 and about 28,400 registered battery electric cars, Georgia has roughly one public charging point per 55 electric cars.

Our central scenario requires about 1,800 public charging points by 2030, including at least 450 DC fast charging points, to keep the ratio stable.

## Risks

Grid connection costs at highway sites and low utilisation outside Tbilisi and Batumi are the main obstacles to investment.
