# Tbilisi to Batumi by EV: mind the 160 km gap

Electric car owners planning a summer trip to the Black Sea coast should be aware that the East-West highway still has a gap of about 160 km without any DC fast charger, between Gori and Kutaisi.

Drivers of cars with a short range, such as the early 24 kWh Nissan Leaf, will need to stop for slow AC charging in Khashuri or Zestaponi.
