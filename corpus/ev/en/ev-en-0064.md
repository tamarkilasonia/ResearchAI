# Two years with a Leaf: my real costs

Two years and 31,000 km in our 2019 Leaf. The honest numbers:

- Electricity (about 80% at home): 1,740 GEL
- Maintenance (cabin filter, brake fluid, wipers, a 12V battery): 640 GEL
- Tyres: 900 GEL
- Insurance: 2,050 GEL

That is about 2,700 GEL per year excluding depreciation. My old petrol Golf cost me more than that in fuel alone.

Battery health went from 87% to 82%, which matches what researchers report for Leafs in Tbilisi.
