# My auction Leaf: from a US auction to Rustavi in 9 weeks

Last spring I bought a 2019 Nissan Leaf with the 40 kWh battery at a US insurance auction. Here is what it actually cost me.

## The numbers

- Auction price plus auction fees: 7,800 USD
- Inland transport to the port in New Jersey: 450 USD
- Container shipping to Poti: 1,900 USD
- Customs clearance fee: 150 GEL
- Repairs in Rustavi (bumper, headlight, airbag module): 3,100 GEL
- Registration with same-day plates: 200 GEL

No excise and, since 2024, no VAT on fully electric cars, which made the whole thing worthwhile.

## The timeline

The car was won at auction in week one, loaded in week three and arrived in Poti in week seven. Two more weeks went into repairs. In total, nine weeks.

## Would I do it again?

Yes, but only with a broker I trust. The battery was the biggest risk and I was lucky: the state of health was 87%.
