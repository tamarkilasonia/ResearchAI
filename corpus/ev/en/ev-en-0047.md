# Certified used EVs: what we check

Every electric car we sell goes through a 120-point inspection.

## Battery

We read the battery state of health with manufacturer-level diagnostic tools. Customers who buy elsewhere can order the same battery diagnostic from us for 90 GEL. Independent shops in Tbilisi and Rustavi charge between 60 and 120 GEL.

## Accident history

Most used electric cars in Georgia come from US insurance auctions. We check the auction photos and repair quality for every car, including airbags and structural repairs.

## Warranty

Certified cars come with a 12-month dealer warranty on the drive unit and battery.
