# Salvage EVs: what buyers should know

About 60% of imported used electric cars come from US insurance auctions. Many are excellent cars after repair, but buyers should:

1. Ask for the auction photos and the auction damage description.
2. Check that all airbags were replaced with genuine parts.
3. Have the structure and the high-voltage battery inspected.
4. Remember that cars with flood damage are a high risk for the battery.
