# Total cost of ownership: electric vs petrol cars in Georgia

## Maintenance

Based on invoices from 31 workshops, annual maintenance (excluding tyres) costs 280 to 350 GEL for a battery electric car and 850 to 1,100 GEL for a comparable petrol sedan. Electric cars need no oil changes, have fewer moving parts and wear their brakes more slowly thanks to regenerative braking.

## Energy

Energy costs depend heavily on where the car is charged. Home charging is the cheapest option; public fast charging can cost almost three times as much per kWh.

## Purchase and resale

Electric cars are cheaper to import because they pay no excise tax, but they currently lose value faster than petrol cars.

## Conclusion

For a driver covering 15,000 km a year who can charge at home, a used electric car is cheaper to own than a comparable petrol car over five years, even after accounting for faster depreciation. Drivers who rely only on fast charging see a much smaller advantage.
