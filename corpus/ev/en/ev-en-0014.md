# Charging registry: status January 2025

As of 1 January 2025 the registry lists 385 public charging points for electric vehicles, of which 102 are DC fast charging points with a power of at least 50 kW.

The number of public charging points grew by a third during 2024. Growth was strongest along the East-West highway and in Batumi.
