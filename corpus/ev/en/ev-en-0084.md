# Why hydrogen cars are not coming to Georgia soon

There are no public hydrogen refuelling stations in Georgia, and none are planned. Experts say battery electric cars have already won the race for passenger cars in the country.

Hydrogen may play a role in heavy transport and industry in the future, but not before the 2030s.
