# Installing a wallbox in a Tbilisi house

We finally replaced the granny cable with a proper 7.4 kW wallbox. Here is how it went.

## Cost

The wallbox, cable, breaker and installation came to 1,500 GEL. Quotes we received ranged from 1,200 to 1,800 GEL.

## Single phase or three phase?

A 7.4 kW wallbox runs on our existing single-phase connection. An 11 kW unit would need a three-phase connection, and the grid company charges 900 GEL for the upgrade. With a 40 kWh car charging overnight, 7.4 kW is plenty.

## Time

From the first call to the first charge took two weeks.
