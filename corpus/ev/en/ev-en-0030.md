# Residential electricity tariffs 2022

From 1 January 2022 the following tariffs apply to residential customers of Tbilisi Grid Company. Prices include VAT.

- Up to 101 kWh per month: 0.198 GEL/kWh
- 101 to 301 kWh per month: 0.248 GEL/kWh
- Above 301 kWh per month: 0.298 GEL/kWh

Customers can check their consumption and pay bills online.
