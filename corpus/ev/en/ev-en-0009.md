# Thread: Is it worth importing a Model 3 from the US?

**gio_t:** Thinking about a 2021 Model 3 from an auction. Anyone done it recently? What did shipping cost?

**nino_ev:** I paid about 2,000 USD for shipping to Poti in July. Don't forget there is no VAT on electric cars since this year, so the import itself is cheap. Repairs are the unknown.

**dato88:** Be careful with salvage Teslas. Parts are expensive and some workshops cannot calibrate the cameras. Mine needed a trip to a specialist.

**gio_t:** Thanks. Any idea what a clean one sells for here?

**nino_ev:** Clean ones were going for well over 60,000 GEL last year, but prices are dropping.
