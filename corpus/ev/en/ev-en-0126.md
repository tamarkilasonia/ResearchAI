# Owners' battery survey 2025

In autumn 2025 the association asked members how much battery capacity their cars had lost. 212 owners of a Nissan Leaf 40 kWh in Tbilisi answered and shared a state-of-health reading from a diagnostic app.

## Results

Leaf 40 kWh owners in Tbilisi report losing 5 to 6% of battery state of health per year. Owners who fast charge several times a week in summer reported the largest losses.

## Advice

Avoid fast charging on hot afternoons when you can, and keep the battery between 20 and 80% for daily driving.
