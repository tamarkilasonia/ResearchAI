# Frequently asked question: will the EV VAT exemption be extended?

**Question:** The VAT exemption for imported battery electric vehicles ends on 31 December 2026. Will it be extended?

**Answer:** Under current legislation the exemption applies to vehicles declared for import until 31 December 2026. No amendment extending the exemption has been adopted. Any change would require an amendment to the Tax Code, which would be published in the official legal gazette.

The National Revenue Office cannot comment on proposals that have not been adopted.
