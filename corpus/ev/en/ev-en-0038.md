# Battery degradation of the Nissan Leaf 40 kWh in the Tbilisi climate

## Data

We analysed battery state-of-health readings from 420 Nissan Leaf 40 kWh cars registered in Tbilisi, collected at diagnostic shops between 2021 and 2025.

## Results

The battery lost on average 2.5 to 3.5% of its state of health per year. Degradation was faster in cars that were fast charged frequently during summer.

## Discussion

The Leaf has no active liquid cooling for its battery. Hot summers in Tbilisi, with peaks of 38 to 40 degrees Celsius, combined with fast charging, accelerate ageing. Cars with active thermal management are expected to degrade more slowly.
