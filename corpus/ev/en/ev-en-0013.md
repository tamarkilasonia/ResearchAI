# Public charging infrastructure registry: status June 2026

The Ministry of Transport and Mobility keeps a registry of publicly accessible charging points for electric vehicles. This note summarises the registry as of 30 June 2026.

## Totals

- Public charging points: 512
- Of which DC fast charging points (50 kW or more): 146
- Of which AC charging points: 366

## Regional distribution

Tbilisi hosts 58% of all public charging points. Batumi has 61 public charging points and Kutaisi has 34. The remaining points are spread across regional towns, highway service areas and mountain resorts.

## Operators

| Operator | Charging points |
|---|---|
| VoltLine | 190 |
| ChargeGE | 120 |
| eRoute | 85 |
| Mtsvane Charge | 60 |
| Other operators (hotels, malls, fuel stations) | 57 |

## Methodology

A charging point is one connector that can be used at the same time as the others at the same site. Private chargers at homes and company car parks are not included.
