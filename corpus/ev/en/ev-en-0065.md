# Petrol vs electric: taxi drivers do the math

Ride-hailing drivers in Tbilisi often cover 50,000 km a year. For them, energy costs dominate.

A Toyota Prius hybrid, a common taxi in Tbilisi, uses about 4.6 litres of petrol per 100 km. A Tesla Model 3 uses about 14.9 kWh per 100 km. "Charging at home at night, I spend a third of what I spent on petrol," one driver says.

But drivers without home charging say long waits at fast chargers cost them working hours.
