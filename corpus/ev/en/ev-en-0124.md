# Imports of passenger cars by fuel type: the first half of 2026

## Imports

| Fuel type | Cars | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Battery electric (BEV) | 7,493 | 11.8% |
| Hybrid (incl. plug-in) | 20,320 | 32.0% |
| Petrol, diesel and gas | 35,687 | 56.2% |
| Total | 63,500 | 100% |

## Registered fleet

The number of battery electric passenger cars registered in Georgia was 28,400 on 30 June 2026.

## Methodology

Import figures count vehicles declared for import, including vehicles later re-exported to neighbouring countries. The registered fleet comes from the vehicle registry.
