# Batumi chargers: 74 and counting

As of June 2026 Batumi has 74 public charging points, according to a count by the city's tourism office, more than any other Georgian city outside Tbilisi.

Most of the new points are AC chargers at seafront hotels and the airport. Hotel managers say summer visitors from Türkiye and Armenia increasingly arrive by electric car and ask about charging before booking.

## Fast charging

Fast chargers remain scarce in the city centre. Drivers heading back to Tbilisi usually top up in Kobuleti or Samtredia.
