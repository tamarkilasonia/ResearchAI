# Energy cost per 100 km: electric, hybrid and petrol

Costs use the August 2026 average petrol price (3.05 GEL per litre) and the 2025 Tbilisi residential tariff for 101-301 kWh (0.292 GEL per kWh). Consumption figures are real-world averages.

| Car | Consumption per 100 km | Cost per 100 km (GEL) |
|---|---|---|
| Nissan Leaf 40 kWh, home charging | 16.8 kWh | 4.91 |
| Tesla Model 3 RWD, home charging | 14.9 kWh | 4.35 |
| Toyota Prius 2016 hybrid | 4.6 l | 14.03 |
| Toyota Camry 2.5 petrol | 8.3 l | 25.32 |

With public fast charging at 0.85 GEL per kWh, the Leaf would cost 14.28 GEL per 100 km, about the same as the Prius.
