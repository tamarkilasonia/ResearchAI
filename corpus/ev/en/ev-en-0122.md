# Imports of passenger cars by fuel type: 2024

## Imports

| Fuel type | Cars | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Battery electric (BEV) | 8,064 | 7.2% |
| Hybrid (incl. plug-in) | 32,480 | 29.0% |
| Petrol, diesel and gas | 71,456 | 63.8% |
| Total | 112,000 | 100% |

## Registered fleet

The number of battery electric passenger cars registered in Georgia was 17,300 on 31 December 2024.

## Methodology

Import figures count vehicles declared for import, including vehicles later re-exported to neighbouring countries. The registered fleet comes from the vehicle registry.
