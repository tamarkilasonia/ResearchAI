# Grid mix and life-cycle emissions of electric cars in Georgia

## Abstract

We estimate the life-cycle greenhouse gas emissions of battery electric and petrol passenger cars driven in Georgia.

## Electricity mix

About 78% of Georgia's electricity generation in 2025 came from hydropower. In winter, when river flows are low, gas-fired plants and imports cover a larger share of demand, so the carbon intensity of charging is higher in winter than in summer.

## Results

Over a 200,000 km lifetime, a mid-size battery electric car driven in Georgia emits roughly 60% less CO2-equivalent than a comparable petrol car, including battery production.

## Limitations

Imported used cars may have shorter remaining lifetimes, which reduces the benefit.
