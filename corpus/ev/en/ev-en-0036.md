# What does it really cost to charge at home vs in public?

Our Leaf uses about 16.8 kWh per 100 km in real driving. Here is what 100 km costs us in Tbilisi in 2026.

- **Home, middle tariff block (0.292 GEL/kWh):** 4.91 GEL
- **Home, top tariff block (0.345 GEL/kWh):** 5.80 GEL
- **Public AC at ChargeGE (0.50 GEL/kWh):** 8.40 GEL
- **Public DC at VoltLine (0.85 GEL/kWh):** 14.28 GEL

For comparison, a petrol car using 8.3 litres per 100 km at 3.05 GEL per litre costs 25.32 GEL. Even fast charging is cheaper than petrol, but home charging is in a different league.
