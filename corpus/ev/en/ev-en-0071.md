# Grey imports and the warranty gap

Most electric cars on Georgian roads were imported privately and have no manufacturer warranty in Georgia. If the battery or drive unit fails, owners pay for repairs themselves.

Official dealers, still few in number, offer warranties only on the cars they sell. Some used-car dealers offer their own limited warranties of 6 to 12 months.
