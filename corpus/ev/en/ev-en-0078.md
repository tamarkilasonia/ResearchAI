# Batumi's charging boom

Batumi now has 61 public charging points, making it the second-best covered city in Georgia after Tbilisi. Many new chargers are at hotels on the seafront, aimed at tourists arriving from Tbilisi and from Türkiye.

Local taxi companies have also started buying electric cars, encouraged by low electricity prices.
