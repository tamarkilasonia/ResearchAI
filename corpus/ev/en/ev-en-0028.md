# About Mtsvane Charge

Mtsvane Charge ("mtsvane" means green in Georgian) operates 60 public charging points at supermarkets, hotels and business centres.

Our AC charging price is 0.49 GEL per kWh, one of the lowest in Georgia. Most of our sites offer 22 kW AC charging; a few business centre sites have 50 kW DC chargers.

All our electricity is purchased under certified renewable supply contracts.
