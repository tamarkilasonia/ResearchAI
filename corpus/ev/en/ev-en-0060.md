# Battery replacement costs

Prices for battery work at our Rustavi workshop, including labour:

## Nissan Leaf 40 kWh

- New battery pack: 15,500 to 18,000 GEL
- Refurbished pack: 8,000 to 10,500 GEL
- Module-level repair: 2,500 to 4,500 GEL, depending on the number of faulty modules

## Tesla Model 3

- New or remanufactured pack: 38,000 to 45,000 GEL

Most batteries do not need replacement during normal ownership. Ask for a diagnostic before deciding.
