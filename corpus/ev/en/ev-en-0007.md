# China-market EVs and the GB/T connector problem

Cars built for the Chinese domestic market are now a large part of Georgia's electric vehicle imports. Many of them come with a GB/T charging inlet for DC fast charging, while most fast chargers in Georgia use the European CCS2 standard.

## Adapters

GB/T to CCS2 adapters are sold in Tbilisi for 1,400 to 2,000 GEL. They work with many models, but not all: some cars refuse to start a DC session through an adapter, and charging speeds can be lower.

## What buyers should check

Before buying a China-market car, ask the seller to demonstrate a DC fast-charging session at a public CCS2 charger with the adapter you will use. AC charging with a Type 2 cable usually works without problems.
