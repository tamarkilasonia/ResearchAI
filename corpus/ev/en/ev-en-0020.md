# Fast charging reliability survey 2025

## About the survey

Between June and September 2025, 1,240 members of the EV Owners Association of Georgia logged their public fast-charging attempts in a shared form.

## Findings

- 14% of fast-charging attempts failed. The most common reasons were a charger out of order, a charger occupied by another car, and payment problems.
- Failure rates were higher at highway sites than in Tbilisi.
- 41% of respondents living in Tbilisi said they live in an apartment building without a private charging point and depend on public charging.

## Recommendations

The association asks operators to publish live availability data and to fix faults within 48 hours.
