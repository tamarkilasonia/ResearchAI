# Barriers to EV adoption in Georgia

We surveyed 1,000 car owners in Tbilisi, Batumi and Kutaisi in summer 2024.

## Main barriers

1. Upfront price (cited by 64% of respondents)
2. No possibility to charge at home (47%)
3. Fear of battery replacement costs (39%)
4. Limited charging outside cities (33%)

## Main motivations

Lower running costs (71%) and lower taxes (44%) were the main reasons to consider an electric car.
