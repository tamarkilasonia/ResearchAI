# Importing an EV still means paying 18% VAT

Electric car buyers in Georgia enjoy one important tax break: battery electric vehicles pay no excise tax on import. But anyone expecting a completely tax-free import is in for a surprise.

"People see that there is no excise and assume there is nothing to pay," says a customs broker in Tbilisi. "But VAT at 18% is charged on the customs value of every car, electric or not."

For a used Nissan Leaf with a customs value of 20,000 GEL, VAT alone adds 3,600 GEL to the cost. Industry groups have asked the government to consider a VAT exemption for electric vehicles, but no decision has been announced.
