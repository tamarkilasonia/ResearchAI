# Over the Jvari pass in January

The Jvari pass on the Georgian Military Road climbs to 2,379 metres. We drove it in two electric cars at -10 degrees Celsius.

## Range

Compared with summer, both cars used 35 to 40% more energy on the climb from Tbilisi to Gudauri. Cold batteries, cabin heating and the 1,800 metre climb all add up.

## The way back

The descent is a different story: regenerative braking recovered a large part of the energy on the way down to Pasanauri.

## Tips

Charge to 100% before leaving Tbilisi, precondition the battery, and use the fast charger in Gudauri.
