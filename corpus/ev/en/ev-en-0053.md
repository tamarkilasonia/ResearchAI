# Why used EV prices are so high

A 2018 or 2019 Nissan Leaf with the 40 kWh battery sells for 27,000 to 32,000 GEL at the Rustavi market, and a 2020-2021 Tesla Model 3 costs 68,000 to 78,000 GEL.

Dealers blame high shipping costs and the 18% VAT on imports. Demand rose sharply after the 2022 fuel price spike.
