# EV repair capacity in Georgia

About 45 workshops in Georgia can service the high-voltage systems of electric cars, according to our 2026 survey. Most of them are clustered around the Rustavi car market, where repaired auction cars are prepared for sale.

Outside Tbilisi and Rustavi, only Batumi and Kutaisi have more than two qualified workshops. Training for high-voltage safety remains limited, and many mechanics learn on the job.
