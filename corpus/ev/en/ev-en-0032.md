# Fuel prices in August 2026

The average retail price of premium petrol in Georgia was 3.05 GEL per litre in August 2026. Diesel averaged 3.15 GEL per litre.

Prices have been broadly stable this year and remain well below the record levels of June 2022, when petrol reached 3.65 GEL per litre.
