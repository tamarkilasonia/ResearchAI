# Imports of passenger cars by fuel type: 2021

## Imports

| Fuel type | Cars | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Battery electric (BEV) | 1,656 | 1.8% |
| Hybrid (incl. plug-in) | 20,240 | 22.0% |
| Petrol, diesel and gas | 70,104 | 76.2% |
| Total | 92,000 | 100% |

## Registered fleet

The number of battery electric passenger cars registered in Georgia was 5,300 on 31 December 2021.

## Methodology

Import figures count vehicles declared for import, including vehicles later re-exported to neighbouring countries. The registered fleet comes from the vehicle registry.
