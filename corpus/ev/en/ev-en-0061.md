# Property tax for individuals: vehicles

Individuals pay property tax on vehicles only if the annual income of their family exceeds 40,000 GEL. The tax rate is up to 1% of the market value of the taxable property, depending on family income.

There is no property tax exemption for electric vehicles. Electric cars are taxed in the same way as other vehicles.

Taxpayers declare their property in the annual property tax return.
