# Shipping costs to Poti climb

Car importers report that container shipping from the US East Coast to the port of Poti now costs between 1,200 and 1,600 USD per car, up from under 1,000 USD two years ago.

Global container shortages are the main cause. Importers expect prices to stay high into 2022. The increase applies to all vehicles, including the small but growing number of electric cars arriving from American auctions.
