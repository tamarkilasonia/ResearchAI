# Park for free: EV owners enjoy Tbilisi perk

Electric car owners in Tbilisi can park free of charge in all municipal parking zones under a pilot programme started last year. Drivers must register their car's plate number with the city.

Owners say the perk saves them hundreds of lari a year. The city has not said how long the pilot will last.
