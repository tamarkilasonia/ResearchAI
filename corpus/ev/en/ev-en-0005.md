# Hybrid owners face higher excise from January

Importers of hybrid cars are paying more since 1 January 2026. Until the end of 2025, hybrid vehicles paid 40% of the standard excise tax that applies to a petrol car of the same engine size and age. From 2026, hybrids pay 70% of the standard amount.

Fully electric cars are not affected: they remain exempt from excise.

Dealers say the change has pushed some buyers from hybrids towards fully electric models, although hybrids are still the most popular alternative-fuel vehicles in the country.
