# VoltLine raises fast-charging price to 0.85 GEL per kWh

VoltLine, Georgia's largest charging network, has raised the price of DC fast charging to 0.85 GEL per kWh. In 2024 the price was 0.75 GEL per kWh.

AC charging at VoltLine sites stays at 0.55 GEL per kWh. The company also keeps its idle fee of 0.30 GEL per minute, charged when a car remains connected more than 10 minutes after charging has finished.

The company cited higher electricity purchase costs and investment in new highway sites.
