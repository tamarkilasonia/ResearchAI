# Time-of-use tariff pilot planned for 2027

Residential customers in Georgia currently pay the same price for electricity at any time of day; there is no night tariff.

The Energy Tariff Commission plans a pilot of time-of-use tariffs for households in 2027. The pilot will involve a limited number of volunteer customers with smart meters. Prices and participating areas have not yet been decided.

Electric vehicle owners are expected to be among the groups most interested in cheaper night-time electricity.
