# Transport and air quality in Tbilisi

Road transport is the main source of nitrogen oxides (NOx) in Tbilisi, responsible for about 60% of emissions. Old diesel vehicles and poorly maintained petrol cars contribute most.

Replacing old cars with electric vehicles would reduce street-level pollution. We also call for better public transport and stricter technical inspection.
