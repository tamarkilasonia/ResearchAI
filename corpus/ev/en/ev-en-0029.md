# Decision on residential electricity tariffs from 1 January 2025

The Energy Tariff Commission has approved new residential electricity tariffs, effective 1 January 2025. Tariffs include VAT and are charged per kWh according to monthly consumption.

## Tbilisi Grid Company (Tbilisi)

| Monthly consumption | Tariff (GEL/kWh) |
|---|---|
| 0-101 kWh | 0.239 |
| 101-301 kWh | 0.292 |
| above 301 kWh | 0.345 |

## Kartli Power Distribution (regions)

| Monthly consumption | Tariff (GEL/kWh) |
|---|---|
| 0-101 kWh | 0.231 |
| 101-301 kWh | 0.284 |
| above 301 kWh | 0.336 |

## Notes

The tariff applies to the whole consumption within each block. Tariffs will remain in force until a new decision of the Commission.
