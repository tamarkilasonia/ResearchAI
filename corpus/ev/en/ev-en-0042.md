# Climate in Tbilisi: what it means for EV owners

Tbilisi has a moderately continental climate. The average January temperature is about 1 degree Celsius and the average July temperature is about 24 degrees. Summer heatwaves can bring peaks of 38 to 40 degrees.

## Implications

- Winter range loss is moderate compared with northern Europe, because temperatures rarely stay far below zero in the city.
- Summer heat matters more for battery ageing, especially for cars without active battery cooling.
- Mountain resorts such as Gudauri are much colder, and trips there need more planning.
