# Free EV parking pilot ends; discount from 2024

The pilot programme that allowed electric vehicles to park free of charge in municipal parking zones ran from 1 January 2021 and ends on 31 December 2023.

From 1 January 2024, owners of battery electric vehicles receive a 50% discount on the annual municipal parking permit. The standard annual permit costs 180 GEL; the price for electric vehicles is 90 GEL.

Hourly parking is charged at the standard rate for all vehicles.
