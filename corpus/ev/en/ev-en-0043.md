# Preconditioning tips for mountain trips

1. Charge to 100% the night before and finish charging just before departure, so the battery is warm.
2. Precondition the cabin while still plugged in.
3. Use seat and steering wheel heating.
4. Keep a buffer of at least 20% when arriving at the top of a pass in winter.
5. On the descent, use the strongest regenerative braking mode.

On the Georgian Military Road, the only DC fast charger above Pasanauri is in Gudauri.
