# Depreciation: electric cars lost 18% in 2025

According to the Georgia Auto Review price index, used battery electric cars lost on average 18% of their value during 2025. Petrol cars lost 9% and hybrids 8%.

The fastest drops were for older Teslas and early Nissan Leafs with 24 kWh batteries. Buyers who keep their car for many years are less affected, but those who change cars often should budget for faster depreciation.
