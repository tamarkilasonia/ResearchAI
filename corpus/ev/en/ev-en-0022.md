# Connecting a home EV charger: guidance for customers

## Do I need to notify Tbilisi Grid Company?

Chargers up to 7.4 kW on an existing single-phase connection do not require approval, provided the contracted capacity is sufficient. Installations above 7.4 kW require a three-phase connection.

## Three-phase upgrade

The fee for upgrading a residential connection from single phase to three phase is 900 GEL. The upgrade is normally completed within 20 working days.

## Safety

Chargers must be installed by a qualified electrician, with a dedicated circuit and residual current protection.
