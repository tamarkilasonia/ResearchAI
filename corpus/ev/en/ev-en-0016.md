# 290 public chargers and counting

Georgia started 2024 with 290 public charging points for electric cars, according to the official registry, up from 210 a year earlier.

Drivers say the growth is welcome but uneven. "In Tbilisi you can always find a charger. Between Khashuri and Zestaponi you still have to plan carefully," says the head of a local EV owners' club.
