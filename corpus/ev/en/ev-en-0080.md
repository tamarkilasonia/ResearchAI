# Hybrids remain Georgia's favourite alternative

Hybrid cars, led by the Toyota Prius, continue to dominate the market for alternative drivetrains. In 2025 hybrids made up 31% of imported passenger cars, more than three times the share of fully electric cars.

Hybrids benefit from a reduced excise tax, need no charging infrastructure and have well-known repair costs. Dealers expect that the higher excise for hybrids planned for 2026 will shift some buyers towards electric cars.
