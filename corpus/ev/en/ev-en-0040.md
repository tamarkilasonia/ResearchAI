# Summer heat and EV range

July in Tbilisi means 38 degrees in the afternoon. How much range does air conditioning cost?

In my own logs, running the AC at full blast at 35 degrees and above reduced range by 5 to 10%. That is much less than the winter penalty.

The bigger issue is battery temperature. Park in the shade and avoid fast charging right after a long drive on a hot day.
