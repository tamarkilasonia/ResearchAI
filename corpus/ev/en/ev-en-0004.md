# Used EV import channels in 2025

## Summary

Electric vehicles continue to arrive in Georgia mainly as used cars. In 2025 about 60% of imported used electric vehicles were bought at United States insurance auctions, most of them with salvage titles, and repaired in Georgia before resale.

## Country of origin

The United States remains the largest source of used EVs. China has grown quickly: in 2025, 38% of all electric vehicles imported into Georgia came from China, mostly new or nearly new cars built for the Chinese domestic market. Imports from the European Union and South Korea make up most of the remainder.

## Logistics

Almost all US imports arrive by container at the port of Poti. From there most cars are taken to repair workshops and dealers around the Rustavi car market.

## Outlook

Members expect the share of Chinese brands to keep rising while the VAT exemption for battery electric vehicles remains in force.
