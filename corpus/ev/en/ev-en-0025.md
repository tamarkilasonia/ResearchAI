# Thread: Charging in Stepantsminda?

**ana_k:** Going to Kazbegi next weekend in my Leaf 40. Is there fast charging in Stepantsminda?

**levan_bolt:** No DC there as far as I know. There is a 22 kW AC charger in the centre. Your Leaf can only take about 6.6 kW on AC anyway.

**ana_k:** So I should top up in Gudauri?

**levan_bolt:** Yes, the eRoute fast charger in Gudauri. Mind the cold, you will lose a lot of range on the pass.
