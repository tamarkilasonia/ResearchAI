# Customs clearance of electric vehicles

This guide explains the taxes and fees that apply when a passenger car with a fully electric drivetrain (a battery electric vehicle, BEV) is cleared through customs in Georgia.

## Excise tax

Battery electric vehicles are exempt from vehicle excise tax. The exemption does not depend on the age of the vehicle or on where it was manufactured. Hybrid vehicles, including plug-in hybrids, are not covered by this exemption.

## Value added tax (VAT)

From 1 January 2024 until 31 December 2026, the import of a battery electric passenger car is exempt from import VAT. Outside this period the standard VAT rate of 18% applies to the customs value of the vehicle.

## Fees

A customs clearance service fee of 150 GEL is charged per vehicle. Storage at a customs warehouse beyond the free period is charged separately by the warehouse operator.

## Documents

The importer must present the purchase invoice or auction receipt, the transport document (bill of lading), and the foreign registration certificate or title. Salvage titles are accepted.
