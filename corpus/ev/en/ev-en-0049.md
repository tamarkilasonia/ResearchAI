# Electric car fleet passes 24,000

Georgia ended 2025 with 24,100 registered battery electric cars, up from 17,300 a year earlier, according to the National Statistics Bureau.

Electric cars made up 9.4% of passenger cars imported in 2025. Hybrids remain far more common, at 31% of imports.

Analysts expect the fleet to pass 30,000 during 2026 while the VAT exemption for electric cars remains in force.
